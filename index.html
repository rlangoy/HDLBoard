No hardware needed

Everything happens on screen, so you can practise anywhere.

Real VHDL and Verilog

The same code you would put on a real FPGA, simulated by GHDL (VHDL) or Icarus (Verilog).

Free for everyone

Open source, and free to use at home or in class.

Flip a switch. Your design reacts.

Switches, pushbuttons, LEDs and 7-segment displays respond to your design while it runs. It feels like the real board, without one on your desk.

Make a mistake. See why in a second.

HDLBoard compiles your design in under a second, while Altera Quartus Prime needs more than five. Mistakes are marked right in your code, with a message that says what is wrong.

Made for the classroom

Built at USN for students taking their first steps in FPGA design. Install it on your own PC, or host one copy and let the whole class use it in the browser.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write

    Edit VHDL or Verilog source files in the browser.

  2. 2

    Simulate

    GHDL (VHDL) or Icarus (Verilog) compiles and runs your design.

  3. 3

    Watch it respond

    Results are drawn live onto the virtual board.

  4. 4

    Interact

    Flip switches and press buttons, and see the design react.
