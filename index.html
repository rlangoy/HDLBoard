Flip a switch. Your design reacts.

Switches, pushbuttons, LEDs and 7-segment displays respond to your design while it runs. It feels like the real board, without one on your desk.

This is real simulation, not a JavaScript imitation: your design runs in GHDL or Icarus Verilog, and every flip of a switch goes to it while it runs.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write

    Edit VHDL or Verilog source files in the browser.

  2. 2

    Simulate

    GHDL (VHDL) or Icarus (Verilog) compiles and runs your design.

  3. 3

    Watch it respond

    Results are drawn live onto the virtual board.

  4. 4

    Interact

    Flip switches and press buttons, and see the design react.

  5. 5

    Take it to the board

    Same pin names as the DE1-SoC: add a pin assignment in Quartus and program it.

Also in the box

  • Real-time pacing: a 1 Hz blink blinks once a second, as on the board
  • Testbenches without ports, with report and assert output live in the console
  • Hints for common mistakes, like did you mean range? for a misspelled keyword
  • Download one file or the whole project as a .zip

What it is not

  • No synthesis, timing analysis or bitstream: use Quartus for the real board
  • One board only: the DE1-SoC’s switches, keys, LEDs and 7-segment displays
  • The browser version starts fresh on reload, so download your files (the Windows app keeps them)
