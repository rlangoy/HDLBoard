Flip a switch. Your design reacts.

Switches, pushbuttons, LEDs and 7-segment displays respond to your VHDL while it runs. It feels like the real board, without one on your desk.

Made for the classroom

Built at USN for students taking their first steps in FPGA design. Install it on your own PC, or host one copy and let the whole class use it in the browser.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write

    Edit VHDL source files in the browser.

  2. 2

    Simulate

    GHDL compiles and runs your design.

  3. 3

    Watch it respond

    Results are drawn live onto the virtual board.

  4. 4

    Interact

    Flip switches and press buttons, and see the design react.
