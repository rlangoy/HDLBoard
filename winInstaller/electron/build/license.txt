GHDL
----

  Version:    5.0.1 (mcode, ucrt64 build for Windows x86-64)
  Copyright:  (C) 2003-2025 Tristan Gingold and the GHDL contributors
  License:    GNU General Public License, version 2
  Source:     https://github.com/ghdl/ghdl
  This release: https://github.com/ghdl/ghdl/releases/tag/v5.0.1

  GHDL is the VHDL analyzer, elaborator and simulator that actually runs
  your designs. HDLBoard starts it as a separate program; it is not
  linked into HDLBoard's own code.

  The complete corresponding source for this GHDL binary is the public
  repository above, at tag v5.0.1.

  GHDL's license text is installed as:

      <installation directory>\resources\ghdl\COPYING

  and the exact release it came from is recorded alongside it in
  VERSION.txt.

  GHDL bundles the IEEE standard VHDL libraries, whose own license is
  installed at resources\ghdl\lib\ghdl\src\ieee2008\LICENSE.
